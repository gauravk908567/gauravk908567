I am a game producer who knows the craft from the inside. I have built games, tested live ones and led teams to ship them: I own the plan, lead the people and get the game into players' hands.

<img src="assets/stars/magha.svg" width="22" height="22" alt="">&nbsp; **Listen first, then lead.** I talk with every team before I plan, so the plan works for the people doing the work.

<img src="assets/stars/mula.svg" width="22" height="22" alt="">&nbsp; **Fix the root, and the branches follow.** I trace a problem to where it starts and fix it there, once.

<img src="assets/stars/shravana.svg" width="22" height="22" alt="">&nbsp; **Say it early, say it clearly.** Nobody on my team is surprised, and nobody is left guessing.
